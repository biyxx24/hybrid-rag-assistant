# About Chinook Records

Chinook Records is a digital music store that sells individual tracks and music videos for
download. The company was founded in 2008 in Calgary, Alberta, where its headquarters remain
today. A second office in Edmonton houses the general management team, and a small technical
operations team works from Lethbridge. Chinook Records is a privately held company with eight
full-time employees.

## What we sell

Our catalogue contains more than 3,500 tracks from roughly 275 artists and 347 albums, organised
into 25 genres that range from Rock, Metal and Jazz to Latin, Classical and TV soundtracks. Every
item in the catalogue is sold per track: there are no streaming subscriptions and no physical
media. Customers in 24 countries have purchased from the store since launch, with the largest
customer communities in the United States, Canada, Brazil, France and Germany.

## How the store works

Every purchase creates an invoice that lists the tracks bought, the unit price of each track and
the billing address of the customer. Invoices are the authoritative record of a purchase: the
customer portal, the refund desk and the sales support agents all work from them. Historical
sales data covers the period from January 2021 to December 2025.

Each customer is assigned a personal sales support agent at the time the account is created. The
agent is the customer's point of contact for order questions, invoice copies and account changes.
Details on reaching your agent are in the Support and Contact guide.

## Company values

Chinook Records was started by two record-store clerks who wanted the catalogue depth of a large
retailer with the personal service of a neighbourhood shop. Three principles still guide the
company:

1. **Fair pricing.** Prices are simple and the same for every customer, regardless of country.
2. **Ownership.** A purchased track belongs to the customer and can be downloaded again at any
   time from the purchase history.
3. **Human support.** Every customer has a named agent, and every support request receives a
   reply from a person, not an automated message.
