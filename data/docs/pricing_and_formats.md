# Pricing and Audio Formats

## Track prices

Chinook Records uses a flat price list. The price does not change with the artist, the album,
the genre or the customer's country.

| Item | Unit price |
| --- | --- |
| Audio track (any genre) | USD 0.99 |
| Music video or TV episode (video file) | USD 1.99 |

Prices are shown and charged in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT, where it applies, is added at
checkout according to the billing address on the invoice.

## Album bundle discount

When a customer adds ten or more tracks from the same album to a single order, the album bundle
discount applies automatically: 15% off the audio tracks of that album. The discount is
calculated per album, so an order that contains two complete albums receives the discount on
both. Video files are excluded from the bundle discount.

## File formats

Every audio track is delivered as an MPEG audio file (MP3) encoded at 320 kbit/s. Older items in
the catalogue were originally sold as Protected AAC audio files; those files have been
re-encoded, and any customer who bought a protected file can download the unprotected MP3 version
from their purchase history at no charge. Purchased AAC audio files from the former iTunes-style
storefront remain available for download in their original format.

Video content is delivered as Protected MPEG-4 video files at 720p. Videos are watchable in the
Chinook Records desktop and mobile apps and in any player that supports the format.

Lossless audio (FLAC) is not part of the standard store. It is offered exclusively to Studio
members of the Chinook Plus programme, described in the Membership Programme guide.

## Previews

A 30-second preview of every track can be played before purchase, without an account. Previews
are streamed at a lower bitrate than the purchased file.

## Re-downloads

Purchased tracks can be downloaded again at any time from the purchase history page. There is no
limit on the number of downloads and no expiry date on access.

## Gift purchases

Tracks can be bought as a gift for another Chinook Records account. The recipient receives an
email with a download link, and the invoice is issued to the purchaser. Gift purchases follow the
same price list and are eligible for the album bundle discount.
