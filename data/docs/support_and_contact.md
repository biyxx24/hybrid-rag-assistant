# Support and Contact

## Your sales support agent

Every Chinook Records customer is assigned a dedicated sales support agent when the account is
created. The agent handles order questions, invoice copies, refund requests, account changes and
membership questions. The name of your agent is shown on the account page and on every invoice,
under "Support representative".

Chinook Records employs three sales support agents, all based in the Calgary office, who report
to the Sales Manager. Agents are assigned by region so that customers in the same country are
usually served by the same person.

## Support hours

| Channel | Hours (Mountain Time) |
| --- | --- |
| Email to your agent | Monday to Friday, 08:00 – 18:00 |
| Live chat | Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 17:00 |
| Phone | Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 16:00 |

Requests sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. Studio members of
Chinook Plus are placed in a priority queue and receive a first reply within four business hours.

## Response times

- Email: first reply within one business day.
- Live chat: typically under five minutes during support hours.
- Refund requests: decision within three business days (see the Refund Policy).

## Escalation

If a request is not resolved by your agent within five business days, you can ask for it to be
escalated to the Sales Manager. Escalations are reviewed within two business days. Technical
problems with downloads or the apps are forwarded by the agent to the IT team in Lethbridge; you
do not need to contact the IT team directly.

## Self-service

Many requests can be completed without contacting support:

- Re-download a purchased track from the purchase history page.
- Download a copy of any invoice as a PDF from the purchase history page.
- Change the billing address, email address or password in the account settings.
- Request a refund directly from an invoice within the 14-day window.
