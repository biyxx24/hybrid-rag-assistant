# Payment and Billing

## Accepted payment methods

Chinook Records accepts the following payment methods at checkout:

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards
- PayPal
- Store balance (prepaid credit added to the account, or credit returned by a refund)

Bank transfers, cheques and cash are not accepted. Purchases made with a card or PayPal are
charged immediately; there is no invoice-later option.

## Currency

All prices are listed and charged in **US dollars (USD)**, regardless of the customer's country.
Card issuers and PayPal convert the amount into the customer's local currency at their own
exchange rate, and some issuers add a foreign-transaction fee. Chinook Records does not charge
any currency conversion fee of its own.

## Taxes

Sales tax, GST or VAT is applied according to the billing address stored on the account at the
time of purchase. The tax amount is shown separately on the invoice. Customers who are exempt from
tax, for example registered businesses in some countries, can upload their exemption certificate
in the account settings; the exemption applies to future purchases only.

## Invoices

Every purchase generates an invoice that lists each track bought with its unit price and
quantity, the subtotal, tax and the total charged. The invoice carries the billing address of the
customer at the time of purchase and the name of the customer's sales support agent. Invoices can
be downloaded as PDF from the purchase history page at any time, and copies can be requested from
your agent.

## Store balance

Credit can be added to the store balance in amounts between USD 5 and USD 100. The balance never
expires and is used automatically for future purchases before any other payment method is
charged. The balance cannot be withdrawn as cash, except where local law requires it.

## Failed or declined payments

If a card payment is declined, no invoice is created and no track is delivered. The most common
causes are an expired card, a mismatch between the billing address and the card issuer's records,
or a spending limit set by the issuer. Updating the card details in the account settings and
trying again resolves most cases.

## Billing for memberships

Chinook Plus fees are charged on the same calendar day each month, to the payment method chosen
when the membership was started. If the charge fails, the membership benefits are paused after a
seven-day grace period until a payment succeeds.
