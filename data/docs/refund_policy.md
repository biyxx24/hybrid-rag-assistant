# Refund Policy

Chinook Records wants every purchase to be the right one. This policy explains when a purchase
can be reimbursed and how to request it.

## Reimbursement window

A refund can be requested within **14 days** of the invoice date. Requests received after the
window has closed are reviewed case by case but are usually declined.

## Eligible purchases

A track or video is eligible for a refund when at least one of the following applies:

- The file has **not been downloaded or played** through the store or the Chinook Records apps.
- The same track was **purchased twice by mistake** (a duplicate purchase). The duplicate is
  refunded even if the file was downloaded.
- The file is **defective**: it does not play, is truncated, or does not match the catalogue
  description. Defective files are refunded or replaced at the customer's choice, with no time
  limit.
- The purchase was made **without the account holder's authorisation**, for example by a child
  or after an account compromise. These cases are handled together with the security team.

Tracks that have been downloaded and played, and that are not defective or duplicates, are not
eligible for a refund. Album bundle discounts are recalculated when part of a bundle is refunded.

## How to request a refund

1. Open the purchase history page and locate the invoice.
2. Select the track(s) and choose **Request refund**, or contact your sales support agent with
   the invoice number.
3. State the reason (not played, duplicate, defective, unauthorised).

The refund desk confirms receipt within one business day and decides within three business days.

## How the money comes back

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds appear on the
statement within **5 to 7 business days** after approval, depending on the card issuer. Refunds
of purchases paid from the Chinook Records store balance are credited back to the balance
immediately.

## Memberships

Chinook Plus subscription fees are not refundable once a billing period has started, but a
subscription can be cancelled at any time and remains active until the end of the paid period.
The free trial can be cancelled before it ends without any charge.

## Gift purchases

A gift purchase can be refunded by the purchaser within the 14-day window as long as the
recipient has not downloaded the track.
