# Chinook Plus Membership Programme

Chinook Plus is the optional membership programme of Chinook Records. Membership is not required
to buy music; it adds discounts, early access and higher-quality audio for frequent buyers.

## Tiers

| Tier | Monthly fee | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listener | Free | Standard store access, previews, re-downloads, wish list |
| Plus | USD 4.99 | 10% off all audio tracks, early access to new releases (48 hours before public release), ad-free previews |
| Studio | USD 9.99 | 20% off all audio tracks, lossless FLAC downloads for every purchase, two complimentary tracks each month, priority support queue |

Every new account starts on the Listener tier. Plus and Studio are billed monthly in US dollars
and can be cancelled at any time; the benefits stay active until the end of the paid month.

## Free trial

New members can try the Plus tier free for 30 days. The trial converts to a paid Plus
subscription unless it is cancelled before the 30 days end. Each account can use the trial once.

## Complimentary Studio tier for top customers

Customers whose lifetime purchases, as recorded on their invoices, exceed **USD 45** receive the
Studio tier free of charge for the following 12 months. Eligibility is checked at the end of
every quarter; qualifying customers are notified by email and by their sales support agent.

## Student discount

Students with a valid university or college email address receive 50% off the Plus tier (USD
2.49 per month). Verification is renewed once a year. The student discount cannot be combined
with the complimentary Studio tier.

## How discounts combine

Membership discounts are applied to the track price before the album bundle discount. A Studio
member buying a complete album therefore pays 20% less on each track and a further 15% less on
the album total. Discounts never apply to video files or to gift purchases.

## Monthly complimentary tracks

Studio members can claim two audio tracks each calendar month at no charge. Unclaimed tracks do
not roll over to the next month. Complimentary tracks appear on an invoice with a unit price of
USD 0.00 and cannot be refunded or gifted.

## Changing or cancelling a membership

Memberships are managed from the account settings page. Upgrades take effect immediately and the
difference is charged pro rata; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the
current billing month. Subscription fees for a billing period that has already started are not
refundable.
